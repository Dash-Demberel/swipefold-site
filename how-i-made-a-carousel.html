How I made this Lisbon carousel in 3 minutes

Six photos from a weekend in Lisbon, one post that flows from slide to slide as you swipe. Here's every step, with the real screens.

Photos 6Slides 6Template Big & smallTime about 3 min
your.account

Swipe it, just like on Instagram.

The same six slides side by side. Pink lines show where Instagram cuts them.

01

Add your photos

I opened Swipefold, chose Seamless carousel and added six photos from the trip: the balcony above the city, dancing in a tiled street, dinner, a beach at sunset, the old stone wall and the sunset viewpoint.

Swipefold lays them out straight away, with each photo running over the cut into the next slide.

02

Tap Remix until it clicks

I didn't pick a template. I tapped Remix a few times. Each tap tries a new template, a background colour taken from my own photos and the caption style that suits it.

I stopped on Big & small with a warm cream from the sunset photos. It looked like a magazine spread.

Like a look but not the colour? Background → From your photos has more colours picked from your pictures.

03

Make the best photo the big one

My favourite was the two of us on the balcony, but the template had put it in a small frame. I tapped it and chose ★ Make it the big one. It swapped places with a big photo.

Drag inside a frame to choose what shows, and pinch to zoom.

04

Preview, export, post

Preview shows the post exactly as it swipes on Instagram. Happy, I tapped Export: all six slides were saved to my photos in order, at Instagram's exact size.

On Instagram: + → Post → Select multiple, pick the slides 1 to 6, keep the crop as it is, and share.

Three tips for a carousel that really joins up

Post in orderSelect the slides 1, 2, 3… in the order Swipefold saved them, or the cuts won't line up.
Don't zoom the cropLeave Instagram's crop exactly as it is. The slides are already the right size (4:5).
Lead with your best photoThe first slide is what people see in the feed. Put a strong photo there and make it the big one.
