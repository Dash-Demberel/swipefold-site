How to tell a story with photos and words

Five photos from Thailand, one or two lines on each, and a last slide that asks people to follow along. A carousel people actually read. Here's every step, with the real screens.

Photos 5Slides 6Template FramedTime about 5 min
your.account

Swipe it, just like on Instagram.

01

Add your photos, then pick a look

I opened Swipefold, chose Photo story and added five photos from the trip, in the order things happened. Each photo becomes its own slide.

Under Template I chose Framed: the photo in a white frame over a soft, blurred copy of itself, with the words underneath. Full photo, Polaroid and Quote are free too.

Under Brand, put your @name on every slide, so a slide shared on its own still leads back to you.

02

Write a line or two for each photo

Tap a slide and write under Text. One or two sentences is enough: what happened, or what you'd tell a friend about that photo.

Stuck? Every slide starts with a sample line that suits your story (travel, food, family, a weekend, a wedding or a celebration), so you can keep it or change it.

03

Finish with an end slide

The last slide asks people to follow along. For a travel story it says Where to next? and Follow the journey. Under End slide you can change the words, choose its photo, or turn it off.

An end slide gives people who swiped all the way through one more thing to do: follow you.

04

Export and post

I tapped Export, and all six slides were saved to my photos in order, at Instagram's 4:5 size.

On Instagram: + → Post → Select multiple, pick the slides 1 to 6 and share. Prefer a Reel? Make video from this story turns it into a video, slide by slide.

Three tips for a story people read to the end

Make slide one a hookThe first slide is all people see in the feed. Start with a line that makes them want the next one.
Keep each slide shortTwo sentences at most. If you need more, it's two slides.
Write the caption lastThe slides tell the story, so the caption can be short: a question, or where it all happened.
