How to turn your photos into a Reel

Eight photos from Thailand and Lisbon, dropped onto a wooden table like real prints, with a title at the start and the end. Made on the phone in a few minutes. Here's every step, with the real screens.

Photos 8Animation Polaroid dropSize 9:16 ReelLength 15 s

01

Add your photos

I opened Swipefold, chose Video and tapped Add your photos. I picked eight: the cliffs at Railay, street food in Bangkok, dancing in Lisbon, the floating market, dinner, the longtail boat, a sunset beach and a rooftop at night.

It starts playing straight away, so you see the video while you change it.

Mix wide shots with close-ups, and people with places. The video feels livelier when no two photos in a row look alike.

02

Pick how the photos move

Under Animate there are lots of ways for photos to move: tiles that swap, a grid that builds up, a slow zoom, a 3D carousel, a shuffling deck and more.

I chose Polaroid drop. Each photo lands on the table as a print and stays there, so by the end the whole trip is piled up in one place. Look changes what's underneath: wood, linen, travel designs or a season.

03

Add a title and music

Under Text I chose Both, for a title card at the start and the end. I wrote Thailand & Lisbon with 2026 underneath, and kept "Until next time" for the closing card. The cards use a blurred version of your first and last photos behind the words.

Under Music, Add music picks a song saved on your phone, and you can choose where in the song it starts.

Want trending audio? Export without music, then add the sound in Instagram when you post.

04

Choose the length, then export

Under Length you can pick 10, 15 or 30 seconds, or Each photo once, which fits the video to your photos. Mine came to 9 seconds of photos, plus the two title cards: 15 seconds in all.

I tapped Export. Swipefold made the MP4 on the phone, 1080 px wide in the 9:16 Reel shape, and saved it to my photos. Then on Instagram: + → Reel, pick the video, and share.

Three tips for a Reel people watch to the end

Keep it short10 to 15 seconds is plenty for 6 to 10 photos. A shorter Reel is more likely to be watched twice.
Open strongPut your best photo first. It's the one people see as the video starts and in your profile grid.
Pick a good coverWhen you post, choose a cover frame where the title or a full photo shows, so the Reel looks good on your profile.

Polaroid drop and most animations are free. Videos made without Swipefold Pro carry a small "Made with Swipefold" mark. Pro removes it and adds the Film strip, Photo river, Before / after, Vision board and Book & calendar animations, plus music beat sync.
