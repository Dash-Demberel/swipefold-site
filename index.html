For Instagram carousels · grid splits · collages · stories · Reels

Posts that come together as you swipe.

Swipefold lays your photos across Instagram slides so each one runs over the cut and only appears whole when you swipe. Add captions, crop, preview, and save every slide at the exact size Instagram wants. Tell the story behind your photos, slide by slide, and turn any of it into a Reel with music.

Every kind of post

Pick a mode, add your photos, and Swipefold handles the cutting, the sizes and the order you upload in.

Seamless carousel

Photos sit across the cuts between slides, so each swipe reveals the rest of the picture. Choose across-cuts, staggered or strip layouts.

Up to 10 slides · 4:5, 3:4 or 1:1

Grid split

One photo becomes several posts that join up on your profile grid. Swipefold numbers them, so post 1 goes up first and the picture lands the right way round.

3×1 to 3×3, plus tall 1×3 · 1080 × 1440 tiles

Collage & boards

Layouts from stacked bands to mosaics, plus vision boards and party boards with a big title in the middle. Photos can be hearts, ovals, arches or torn paper, with stickers on top.

Post or Story sizes · drag to crop each photo

Tell the story behind the photos

One photo and a few lines on every slide. Pick a look you love, write as you go, and finish with a slide that asks people to follow along.

A look for every story

Framed, magazine, polaroid, film frame, scrapbook, bold banners, quiet text-only slides and more. Switch any time and every slide follows.

Your name on every slide

Add your name or account and a small logo, so your posts are recognisable wherever they're shared.

Backgrounds that set the mood

Illustrated designs for travel, weddings, birthdays and holidays, one of your own photos, or a clean plain colour behind every slide.

Words to get you started

Sample lines for travel, food, family and celebrations fill each slide until you write your own.

Post it or play it

Save the slides for a carousel, or turn the whole story into a video with your own pace, transitions and music.

Feed or Stories shape

Make tall slides for the feed, square ones, or full-screen slides for Stories and Reels.

Boards, books and calendars

Put your photos on a vision board, a birthday board or a travel board, or flip through them like a book. In the video, the title comes first and every photo lands in its own spot, so nothing overlaps.

Reels from your photos

Choose an animation and your photos become a short video, ready for Reels, Stories or a post. Add a song from your phone, and Swipefold can change photos right on the beat.

Lots of ways to move

Swap tiles, Build up, Zoom in, Drift, Polaroid drop, Photo river, Film strip, 3D carousel, Shuffle deck, Vision board, Book & calendar, Split reveal and Before / after.

Music and beat sync

Pick a song and where it starts. Swipefold finds the tempo, fades out at the end and saves the sound into the video.

Backdrops for every occasion

Oak, marble, linen and brick, illustrated designs, plus seasons and holidays from Halloween to St Patrick's Day. Or use a photo of your own table.

Carousel to video

Any carousel becomes a video in one tap: a swipe through the slides, or a smooth glide that slows down on each photo.

The fiddly parts, done for you

Small things that usually take ages to line up by hand.

Captions find the space

Tick one box and every photo gets a caption, placed in the biggest empty space beside it, on the slide that holds most of the photo.

Opens and ends on a full photo

The first photo starts at the top of slide 1 and the last one rests on the bottom of the final slide, each showing at least 70% of itself.

Crop inside the frame

Zoom and drag a photo inside its frame to choose exactly what shows, or set any shape you like.

Exact Instagram sizes

Slides export at 1080 × 1350, and grid tiles at 1080 × 1440 to match the 3:4 profile grid, so nothing gets trimmed.

Presets and saved projects

Save a look you like and reuse it in one tap. Your work saves itself, so you can pick up where you left off.

Straight to Photos or Instagram

Save every slide to your photo library in order, or open the share sheet and send them to Instagram.

Your photos stay on your phone.

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no servers. Swipefold only sees the photos you pick, and everything happens on your device. Read the privacy policy
