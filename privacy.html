Privacy policy

Swipefold collects nothing.

Last updated 24 October 2026

Swipefold lays out your photos into Instagram carousels, grid splits and collages, and turns them into short videos. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Swipefold app on Android or iOS, or the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. Swipefold has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Your photos and music

Photos and songs you pick stay on your device. They are used only to build the images and videos you export. Saved projects and presets are stored on your device, inside the app, and are deleted if you delete the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): to save your finished slides, collages and videos to your photo library.
  • Photo picker: to choose the photos you want to lay out. Swipefold only sees the photos you select.
  • Files: to choose a song for a video, only when you tap Add music. Swipefold only sees the file you select.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet sends the images or video to the app you choose, for example Instagram. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

Swipefold doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
