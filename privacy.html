Privacy policy

Swipefold collects nothing about you.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Swipefold lays out your photos into Instagram carousels, grid splits and collages, and turns them into short videos. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Swipefold app on Android or iOS, or the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. Swipefold has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Places on the Travel map

The Travel map video can show where your photos were taken. The location saved in a photo is read on your device and matched to a list of towns built into the app, so photo locations never leave your phone.

When you type a place name, town suggestions also come from that built-in list. If you're online, Swipefold also suggests landmarks such as beaches, lighthouses or castles. For this, the words you type and the rough area of your trip (to about 10 km) are sent to Photon, a free place search run by komoot GmbH in Germany using OpenStreetMap data. Nothing else is sent: no photos, no photo locations, no name or device ID. Photon doesn't need an account and Swipefold doesn't keep the searches. komoot's own privacy policy applies to its server. Landmark data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Your photos and music

Photos and songs you pick stay on your device. They are used only to build the images and videos you export. Saved projects and presets are stored on your device, inside the app, and are deleted if you delete the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): to save your finished slides, collages and videos to your photo library.
  • Photo picker: to choose the photos you want to lay out. Swipefold only sees the photos you select.
  • Files: to choose a song for a video, only when you tap Add music. Swipefold only sees the file you select.
  • Photo locations (Android, "Add photos with their places"): lets Swipefold read where the photos you select were taken, so the Travel map can place them. It's used only on your device.
  • Internet: used only for the optional landmark suggestions described above.

Purchases

If you buy Swipefold Pro, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app only asks the store whether Pro is active on your account; we never see your name, email or payment details.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet sends the images or video to the app you choose, for example Instagram. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

Swipefold doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
